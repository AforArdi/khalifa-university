Undergraduate Admissions

For high school graduates (HSC/A-Level/IB) seeking a Bachelor's degree.

Scholarship Tiers

International students compete for highly selective merit-based scholarships.

President's Scholarship The most prestigious award. Full coverage plus generous monthly stipend.
KU Scholarship (Tier 1) Full tuition waiver. Stipend amount may vary or be partial compared to President's.

Required Documents

  • ● Attested High School Certificates (Ministry of Foreign Affairs)
  • ● IELTS (6.0+) or TOEFL (79+)
  • ● SAT Math Score (Typically 700+)
  • ● Statement of Purpose (SOP)
  • ● Passport Copy

Important Note on Competitiveness

Khalifa University is highly competitive. Meeting the minimum requirements listed here does not guarantee admission or a scholarship. Successful applicants typically exceed these minimums significantly (e.g., SAT Math 750+, Golden GPA 5.0).

Postgraduate (MSc & PhD)

Comprehensive funding for research-oriented Master's and Doctorate students.

Funding & Benefits: GRTA Scholarship

The Graduate Research / Teaching Assistant (GRTA) scheme is the primary funding source for international full-time students.

Core Benefits (Both)

  • • 100% Tuition Waiver
  • • Medical Insurance (UAE Visa coverage)
  • • Support for International Research Conferences
  • • Annual Flight Ticket home

PhD Specifics

Higher monthly stipend. Requires a well-defined research proposal and potential supervisor agreement prior to admission (recommended).
